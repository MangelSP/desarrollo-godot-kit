# Knowledge sources & credits

The cards under `knowledge/` are **the repo owner's own study summaries** of the books
below — distilled into actionable rules, numbers, checklists and anti-patterns for AI
agents building 2D games in Godot 4. They are notes *about* these works, not copies of
them. Every card names its source book, author, chapter and pages in its `sources:`
field so credit is traceable to the exact origin.

All credit for the underlying ideas belongs to these authors. If you find these notes
useful, **buy the original books** — the summaries are no substitute for the real thing.

Each `sources:` entry also records the chapter and page range the card was drawn from.

## Nature of these notes

- They contain **facts, rules, numbers and checklists** — e.g. "3–5 shades per material
  ramp", "camera look-ahead ≈ 20–30% of the screen toward velocity". Facts and ideas are
  not copyrightable; the expression in the original books is.
- They are written in the summarizer's own words, reorganized for agent use, not
  transcribed from the source text.
- Some cards carry a `> CHECK:` line where the summarizer's OCR/AI pass was uncertain —
  those are notes to self, not claims about the book.

## Takedown

These are the repo owner's personal summaries, published in good faith as study notes
with full attribution. **If you are an author or rights holder of one of the books above
and want your material's summary removed, open an issue or contact the repo owner and it
will be taken down promptly.**
